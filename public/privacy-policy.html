Privacy Policy

HR Smart Solutions — by Saiful Islam (Sumir)

📋 Overview

HR Smart Solutions is a free utility app for Maternity Benefit and Final Settlement calculation, Wages Grid, file sharing and video calling.

This privacy policy explains how we handle your information. In short: we, the developer, do not collect or store your data on any server of ours. The optional Share File feature stores files in your own Google Drive, as described below.

🔒 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking
  • We do not show advertisements
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone
  • We do not require an account to use the calculators (a Google sign-in is needed only for Share File)

💾 Local Storage Only

The app stores your calculation history only on your device using browser localStorage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never sent to any server
  • Can be cleared anytime from within the app
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

☁️ Share File & Google Drive

If you choose to use Share File, Google shows its own sign-in and consent screen. The app requests only the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files and folder it creates itself in your Google Drive — never your other files, contacts or e-mail.

  • The file or text you pick goes straight from your device to your own Google Drive (folder “HR Smart Solutions - Shared”). It does not pass through any server of ours.
  • To create a link, the app sets that file to “anyone with the link can view”. Anyone who has the link can download it. You can switch this off at any time with “Stop sharing” in the app, or from Google Drive.
  • The Google access token is kept in memory only and is discarded when you close the app or tap Disconnect. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to files stored in Google Drive.
  • HR Smart Solutions’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

📹 Video Call

Video Call connects two browsers directly (WebRTC). We do not record or store calls. Setting up the connection may use public network relay servers operated by third parties; the audio and video are encrypted in transit.

📤 WhatsApp Sharing

When you tap the WhatsApp share button, the app opens the WhatsApp application on your device with a pre-formatted message. We do not access your WhatsApp account, contacts, or messages in any way.

🌐 Internet Usage

The app loads Google Fonts (Inter, Hind Siliguri, Press Start 2P) when online. No user data is transmitted during this process.

👶 Children's Privacy

HR Smart Solutions is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new version here. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

⚖️ Your Rights

  • We hold no personal data on our servers, so there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our end; files in your Google Drive are yours to manage or delete
  • You can clear your local history anytime within the app
  • You can uninstall the app at any time
